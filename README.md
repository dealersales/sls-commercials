# SLS Commercials — design preview

Static export of the DSC dealer demo for SLS Commercials, published at https://dealersales.github.io/sls-commercials/.
Generated by `../export_static.py` from the PHP build — do not edit these files by hand.

Stock, prices and vehicle photographs are SLS's own, harvested from slscommercials.co.uk on the export date. Finance is described through their own introducer-appointed-representative disclosure; no APR, monthly payment or warranty claim appears anywhere because SLS publish none. Hours, address and phone come from their Google Business Profile and their own site.

This is a preview, not a shopfront: noindex + robots.txt disallow, and it must not be pointed at a client domain.
